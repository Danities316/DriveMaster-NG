# Home page rationale

The structure applies the four parts of Alex Hormozi's value equation: desired outcome, confidence in achieving it, time to value, and required effort. Reference: [Acquisition.com value checklist](https://www.acquisition.com/hubfs/Offer%20Checklists%20-%20PDF%20Downloads/Pricing-Value-Checklist.pdf?hsLang=en).

- **Outcome:** clear student/payment records and less administrative searching. The headline and benefit sections express this in the school's language.
- **Confidence:** a labeled product preview, an interactive payment example, and specific explanations of pending versus confirmed work. No fabricated testimonials, adoption numbers, revenue claims, or endorsement.
- **Time to value:** the example is available immediately without registering or contacting anyone.
- **Effort:** three visible workflow steps, familiar naira amounts, keyboard-accessible controls, mobile navigation, and a simple existing-account sign-in route.

The page describes student/payment/synchronization workflows plus vehicle mileage, fuel recording, full-tank consumption and owner-configured discrepancy flags. A dedicated responsive mileage-and-fuel section explains the workflow with a clearly labelled illustrative comparison: 75 litres over 500 km is 15 L/100 km, 25% above a 12 L/100 km limit. Navigation, hero copy, feature strip, FAQ and closing copy include the feature. Pricing, self-service school registration, lesson scheduling and claim approvals are not presented as available. There is no lead form pretending to submit a request. A verified business contact or booking destination can be added when supplied by the owner.

These are design decisions inspired by the framework, not a claim that Alex Hormozi recommends or endorses DriveMaster NG. Conversion improvements require measurement with real visitors; no conversion lift has been established.


## Blue brand and simple English

Primary colour is #0000FF (RGB 0, 0, 255), with white button text, pale blue supporting surfaces and amber for warnings. Copy focuses on fees paid, money owed and vehicle fuel use. “Set fuel limit” replaces “Set benchmark”; sending records is explained in everyday language. The value model is applied through clear outcomes, labelled working examples, a no-sign-in demonstration, and short practical steps. No savings guarantees, invented testimonials or automatic fraud-detection claims are used.
